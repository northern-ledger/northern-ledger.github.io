Privacy Policy

Northern Ledger Automation: what Google and YouTube data it accesses, why, and how it is protected.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short

  • Northern Ledger Automation is a private application with one user: its operator. It has no public accounts.
  • It uses YouTube API Services, through Google OAuth, to upload videos the operator has approved to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • It never asks for or stores a Google password, and it never reads comments, messages, analytics or subscriber data.
  • It does not sell Google user data, share it for advertising or use it to train AI models.
  • The operator can revoke access at any time in the Google Account settings.

1. Who operates the application

Northern Ledger Automation (the "Application") is operated by Northern Ledger (the "operator") for the operator's own use. It is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the operator signs in to it or authorizes it. Questions can be sent to actuality.ro@gmail.com.

2. Google OAuth and YouTube API Services

The Application uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the Application, the operator agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access is granted by the operator on Google's own consent screen. The Application never sees or stores the operator's Google password.

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Google user data the Application accesses

The Application requests exactly two OAuth scopes and nothing else. It does not request the operator's name, email address, contacts, files or any other Google service.

"Manage your YouTube videos" (youtube.upload)
Used only to upload a video that the operator has approved to the operator's own channel. What is sent to YouTube: the video file, its title, description and tags, its category, its privacy setting (unlisted) and the declaration that it is not made for kids. What comes back: the ID of the new video, which the Application records and links to in its confirmation message.
"View your YouTube account" (youtube.readonly)
Requested together with the upload permission, because the upload tool asks for both. At present the Application does not use it to read anything from the channel. It is reserved for a planned check, described in section 4, that would read the channel ID and channel name.
The authorization itself
Google issues the Application an authorization (an OAuth refresh token and short-lived access tokens). Their storage is described in section 7.

4. Video uploads and channel identity

  • Uploads. A finished video is sent to the operator for review in Telegram. Only after the operator explicitly approves that exact video, with its title, description and tags, is it sent to YouTube. If the file is changed after review, the upload is refused. The upload is set to unlisted. The Application does not publish videos as public and never uploads without approval.
  • Which channel. The operator chooses the channel on Google's consent screen when granting access. The Application does not currently re-check which channel an authorization belongs to before each upload.
  • Interrupted uploads. If an upload is interrupted and it is unclear whether the video arrived, the Application stops, marks the upload as needing a check and asks the operator to look at the channel. It never retries automatically and does not fetch the channel's videos.
  • Planned, not yet active. Before every upload the Application will read the channel ID from YouTube and compare it with the channel registered by the operator. For interrupted uploads it will list the channel's most recent uploads (ID, title, publication time, tags, privacy status) for the operator to review. This policy will be updated before these features are switched on.

5. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, which are visible to the operator in the Application. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or any other purpose, and it is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. No person other than the operator reads it, except where needed for security, to comply with law, or with the operator's consent.

6. Sharing with third parties

The Application does not sell Google user data and does not transfer it to third parties, with these exceptions:

  • Google and YouTube receive the uploads and requests described above, as that is the purpose of the Application.
  • Telegram. The Application sends review and status messages to the operator through a Telegram bot: the finished video, its title, description, tags and privacy setting, and, after an upload, a link to the new video. They are delivered only to the operator's own Telegram chat and are handled by Telegram under its own privacy policy.
  • AI assistant, only at the operator's request. The operator can chat with an AI assistant through the Application's Telegram bot. The Application forwards only the operator's own messages and adds no Google data to them. If the operator asks the assistant to look at the YouTube channel, what it reads becomes part of that conversation and is processed by the assistant's AI provider.
  • Security or law. Information may be disclosed where required by law or to investigate abuse.

The Application's content pipeline also uses third-party AI and speech services to help research, write and narrate video content. They receive news material and draft scripts. The Application's pipeline does not send them Google credentials or any data obtained from Google or YouTube.

7. How OAuth credentials are stored and protected

  • Credentials are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are never uploaded to a website, cloud storage or any third party, and they are used only to communicate with Google's servers.
  • They are kept in files whose operating-system permissions allow only the operator's own computer account to read them.
  • Within the Application the credential is used only by the upload step, which runs only after the operator has approved a specific video.
  • Planned, not yet active: moving the credential into a separate, encrypted store readable only by a dedicated service account. This policy will be updated when that is in place.

8. What is stored and for how long

The Application keeps operational records on the operator's computer. For each video these are its title, description and tags, the finished video file, checksums, timestamps, the privacy setting, the approval and upload status, any error message from an upload, and the video ID returned by YouTube. The Application does not store the channel's ID or name, and it does not copy the channel's other videos, comments or statistics.

Video files and records are kept until the operator deletes them; the Application does not delete them automatically.

Messages sent through Telegram (section 6) remain in the operator's Telegram chat until the operator deletes them and are retained by Telegram according to its own policy.

9. Revoking access and deleting data

  • The operator can revoke the Application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, also reachable through Google's security settings page. Select "Northern Ledger Automation" and choose "Remove access". The stored credential stops working immediately.
  • When access is revoked, the operator deletes the stored credential and the Application's stored records for that channel.
  • In addition to the Application's normal deletion procedure, revoking access through the Google security settings page is always available.

10. Children

The Application is not directed at children and has no users other than its operator.

11. This website

This website is a static information page. It has no sign-in, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising and loads nothing from third parties. Like any web host, the hosting provider may keep technical server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policy.

12. Changes to this policy

If the Application's use of Google user data changes, this policy is updated before the change takes effect and the date at the top is revised.
